**Address search.** When you search for an address for an event, the search text goes directly from your phone to the Norwegian Mapping Authority (Kartverket) address service. Kartverket does not receive who you are from us.

## 3. Who can see what

- **People in your hubs** see your name, photo, verified badge, your messages, reactions, RSVPs and notes in that hub, and that you are a member.
- **Hub admins** can also approve or remove members and remove messages in their hub.
- **People you block** no longer see your messages, and you no longer see theirs.
- **Anyone who has your calendar feed link** can see the events in it. Treat it like a password; you can make a new link at any time, which stops the old one.
- **Us**, to run the service and to handle reports. We look at content only when it has been reported, or when needed to fix a fault or meet a legal duty.

## 5. How long we keep data

- **Your account** is kept until you delete it. You can delete it at any time under Profile → Delete account.
- **When you delete your account** we delete at once your name, phone number, email, Vipps ID, birth year, photo, memberships, RSVPs, reactions, read status, push tokens, calendar feed, blocks and sign-in sessions. Hubs you own where you are the only member are closed and deleted. **Messages you wrote in a hub stay in that hub, shown as from "Deleted user"**, because they are part of a conversation others take part in. If you want them gone, delete them before you delete your account.
- **A message you delete** has its text and pictures removed at once.
- **A hub that is closed** is deleted with its chat, events and pictures.
- **Push notification records** are deleted after 30 days.
- **Reports** are kept for 12 months after they are handled.
- **Server logs** are kept by our providers for a limited time, normally up to 30 days.
- **Backups** may hold deleted data for up to **[XX] days** before it is overwritten.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and move (data portability) your data, to restrict processing, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can see and change most of your data in the app, and delete your account yourself. For anything else, email personvern@hubbr.no.

You can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.no) if you think we process your data unlawfully.

## 7. Age limit

Hubbr is only for people aged 18 or over. We check age through Vipps at sign-in.

## 8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored in the EU. Access to the production database is limited to the people who run Hubbr.

## 9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
